## 0.11.0

### What's new
- Map card: **full screen** button, under the zoom buttons (`show_fullscreen` option, on by default). On iPhone, where Safari and the app don't allow an element to go full screen, the map covers the whole page. Escape or the same button closes it; the stations are fitted again on every size change.

## 0.10.0

### What's new
- Map card: new **Home Assistant** background (`map_style: ha`), now the default. It is the background of Home Assistant's native map from 2026.9: OpenStreetMap vector tiles and a light or dark style served by your instance, following the theme live.
- Without WebGL2, or with a Home Assistant version older than 2026.9, the map falls back to OpenStreetMap raster tiles.
- MapLibre GL 6.14.0 and its Leaflet adapter 0.1.4 (Home Assistant's versions) ship with the integration.

## 0.8.1

### Fixes
- A fuel could disappear from a station (tiles and entities) when the feed published neither a price nor a shortage, for example right after a shortage ended, before a new price was entered. A fuel that already had entities now stays tracked: it shows **No price** (with "shortage ended … ago" when the feed says so), and its price and availability are unknown until the next published price.
- The raw shortage details of the feed are read, to detect a shortage that just ended (`rupture_end` attribute).

## 0.8.0

### Changes
- AI names removed (Gemini or another `ai_task` entity): the community names file makes them unnecessary. The option and the names obtained by AI are removed from the configuration automatically at startup.
- Name order: custom name, community file, OpenStreetMap, brand and town, address.

## 0.7.1

### Changes
- The community file also becomes the main source of the brand; OpenStreetMap is only a fallback, and still used for the logo.

## 0.7.0

### What's new
- Stations take their official name from the community file `stations_name.json` of the [hass-prixcarburant](https://github.com/Aohzan/hass-prixcarburant) project (about 9,200 stations, updated weekly): for example "Relais les Trois Marches" instead of "Route de Lorient Vezin-le-Coquet". Upper-case names are formatted.
- The file is downloaded at most once a week and cached: without access to GitHub, the cached copy is used.
- Name order: custom name, community file, OpenStreetMap, AI, brand and town, address. AI is no longer asked for stations found in the file.
- The file's brand is used when OpenStreetMap has none.

## 0.6.0

### What's new
- **AI for missing names** option: a Home Assistant AI entity (Google Gemini or another, through `ai_task.generate_data`) is asked for the name of the stations OpenStreetMap doesn't know. Each station is asked once; an uncertain answer is ignored and the automatic name is kept. Turning on Google search on the Gemini entity gives verified names rather than guesses.
- Name order: custom name, then OpenStreetMap, then AI, then brand and town, then address.

## 0.1.0

First public release of the Carburants France integration for Home Assistant, with its Lovelace card.

### What's new
- Fuel prices and availability from the v2 real-time feed of data.economie.gouv.fr, without an API key, in a single request for all stations.
- Setup from the UI: choice of fuels (Diesel, SP95, SP98, E10, E85, LPG), then search for stations around home, by postcode, town, department or ID, with prices and distance shown in the list.
- Several searches in the same form; the selection is kept.
- Options: fuels, tracked stations, adding stations, update interval (15 minutes by default, 5 minimum).
- **Price** sensors (€/L) and **Available** binary sensors per station and fuel, with the type and start date of shortages.
- **Best price** sensor per fuel among the tracked stations.
- Brand and logo of each station found through OpenStreetMap and Wikidata, retried at startup when it failed.
- `carburants_france.refresh` action to force an update.
- `custom:carburants-france-card` card bundled with the integration and added to the dashboard resources automatically: logo, name, address, distance and live opening status (open, closed, 24/7, 24/7 automat), price of each fuel with its age, shortages in orange, best price in green, details on click (opening hours, services, directions), sorting by price, distance, name or configuration order, full visual editor, light and dark themes.
- Installation through HACS (custom repository, Integration category) or by copying `custom_components/carburants_france/`; `carburants_france.zip` is attached to the release.
- French and English.
